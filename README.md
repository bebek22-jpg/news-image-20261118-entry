Entry for the [make.fun bounty "Sum up 18 November's world news in one image"](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261118): a newspaper front page that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026 in a single 1024×1024 image.

## What the front page covers

**Lead story — the Gulf:** the United States struck an Iranian missile site on Qeshm Island, and Iran fired drones at U.S. bases in Bahrain and Kuwait; both Gulf states said their air defences intercepted the attacks.

**The rest of the day:**

- **Ukraine** — the Kerch Bridge to Crimea hit for a second day; part of its road span fell into the strait.
- **China** — Xi Jinping, Vladimir Putin and Donald Trump to meet in Shenzhen on Thursday, on the sidelines of APEC.
- **Philippines** — Trump meets the Quad leaders in Manila; the four countries agreed steps to secure critical-mineral supplies.
- **United States** — Democrat Mary Peltola wins Alaska's Senate seat, giving Democrats 56 seats.
- **Congo** — the Ebola outbreak more than doubles: over 11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths in three months.
- **Pacific** — Category 5 Cyclone Xavier slams the Northern Cook Islands with gusts up to 315 km/h.
- **Russia** — a military An-12 transport plane crashes in Siberia, killing all 19 on board.
- **United States** — America loses its measles-free status after 26 years: more than 5,400 cases and 10 deaths.
- **Markets** — Nvidia tops forecasts with $115.8bn revenue, but the Dow falls nearly 850 points after an NSA network outage.

Also noted: Israel's parliament elects an Eisenkot ally as speaker, cold kills displaced families in Gaza tents, and the World Court is to rule on the Myanmar genocide case on Thursday.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the pre-drawn `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (default `/output`). The image was drawn from scratch with PIL: crisp text, no network, and the program writes the exact same bytes on every run.
